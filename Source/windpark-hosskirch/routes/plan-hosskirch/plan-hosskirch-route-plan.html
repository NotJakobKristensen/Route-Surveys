How this plan was made

The road graph of Germany (Overture Maps transportation, derived from OpenStreetMap: motorways to secondary roads, every road within about 20 km of the start and the end). Roads whose signed weight, height, length or width limit this convoy exceeds are left out. Up to four distinct routes are found under a heavy-transport policy: motorways, trunk and federal roads on the long haul, town streets of 50 km/h or less avoided, minor roads only near the ends, Germany only (plus the Goleniów – Szczecin area).

Each candidate is driven with the swept-path model of this blade's convoy (tractor, front support, remote-steered rear dolly, the blade's real plan shape) on every bend under 700 m radius, with carriageway widths by road class. It is also checked for town streets, road bridges over it (from the map), tunnels, signed limits close to the convoy's, mapped narrow roads, and today's roadworks and lane restrictions on the motorways (Autobahn GmbH). The score is the convoy's driving time in minutes plus a time-equivalent for each challenge (weights below).

Limits

A screening, not a permit route. Carriageway widths here are typical widths for the road class. The full survey (survey_route.py survey) measures the real edges on 20 cm aerial photos, clear heights from laser terrain and point clouds, and every sign and map tag. Railway bridges and footbridges over the route are not in the bridge count. Roadworks are a snapshot of the day the plan was made. Restrictions that are signed but not mapped are unknown.
