How this was built

Every object sits at its mapped position in a local transverse Mercator frame centred on the park (47.9595° N, 9.4452° E, WGS84).

  • Turbines: OpenStreetMap power=generator nodes (7-decimal coordinates, MaStR IDs, commissioning dates from start_date), cross-checked against the crane pads and tower glint in Sentinel-2 imagery. Hub 175 m, rotor Ø 172 m (86 m along each blade; with the 6° cone and pre-bend the swept circle at rest is Ø 170 m), upper blade tip 261 m, Max Bögl hybrid tower (103 m concrete), AVV 2020 marking and lights. Tower-foot elevations: German Wikipedia.
  • Terrain: Copernicus GLO-30 surface model with the forest canopy removed and corrected inside the forest to the published tower-foot elevations; crane pads and foundations levelled with embankments, lakes at their GLO-30 water surface; fine 3–8 m terrain over the pads and lakes.
  • Map features: roads, tracks, buildings, forest, water, orchards, hedges and the 110 kV lines from OpenStreetMap via Overture Maps (2026-09-23), building heights from OSM and Microsoft ML.
  • Ground colour: Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A surface reflectance of 19 June 2026, the day before the park's opening ceremony; the same scene traces the crane-pad clearings and marks clear-cuts and young growth in the forest.
  • Horizon: terrain to ±64 km and the Alpine skyline to 260 km from AWS Terrain Tiles (Earth curvature and refraction included); forests and towns out there from ESA WorldCover 2021.

Keys: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, W A S D to move, Q / E to turn, R / F to tilt, + / − to zoom, H to hide the panels.
